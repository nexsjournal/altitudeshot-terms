2. Services

2.1 Service Description

AltitudeNow is an outdoor check-in and summit commemoration tool centered on "the moment you arrive." It mainly provides the following services:

1. Real-time altitude and place display

  • Display current altitude based on device GPS (supports meters/feet switching)
  • Display human-readable place text when signal is available (such as landmarks or area names)
  • Provide clear status prompts when accuracy is insufficient or signal is weak

2. Summit camera and card generation

  • Full-screen camera preview with overlaid altitude, place, time, and other information
  • Multiple summit card templates (such as minimal arrival and data cards)
  • Combine captured images with data into shareable summit cards

3. Card export and sharing

  • Preview generated results
  • Save images to the device photo library (requires your authorization)
  • Share to social platforms or other apps through the system share feature

4. Arrival record management

  • View historical arrival records on a timeline
  • View details for individual records (altitude, place, time, images, etc.)
  • Persist records locally on the device and support deletion

5. Settings and auxiliary features

  • Altitude units and privacy-related explanations
  • On supported devices, read barometric and related sensor data for on-screen display or weather ambience (does not replace GPS altitude as the primary data source)
  • Obtain weather summaries and related environmental information over the network when needed (for cards or on-screen display, with fallback when unavailable)

2.2 Service Characteristics

  • Local-first: Arrival records, summit cards, and related data are stored on your device by default
  • No registration required: Core features can be used without creating an account
  • Offline capable: Altitude and GPS-related features work without a network; features that depend on the network such as reverse geocoding and weather queries may degrade or become unavailable
  • Current version: Primarily free to use; if premium templates, subscriptions, or other paid features are offered in the future, they will be described separately and shown in the app
