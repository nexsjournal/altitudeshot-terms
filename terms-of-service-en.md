## 2. Services

### 2.1 Service Description

AltitudeNow is an outdoor check-in and summit commemoration tool centered on "the moment you arrive." It mainly provides the following services:

1. **Real-time altitude and place display**
   - Display current altitude based on device GPS (supports meters/feet switching)
   - Display human-readable place text when signal is available (such as landmarks or area names)
   - Provide clear status prompts when accuracy is insufficient or signal is weak
   - Display barometric pressure and estimated oxygen content on supported devices

2. **Compass**
   - Full-screen compass displaying bearing, latitude and longitude, and current altitude

3. **Summit camera and card generation**
   - Full-screen camera preview, or switch to a theme gradient background for one-tap Pin
   - Overlay altitude, place, time, pressure, and other information
   - Multiple summit card templates
   - Combine captured or generated results into shareable summit cards

4. **Track recording**
   - Full-screen map showing current location, heading, and live route
   - Start/pause/resume/end recording with distance, speed, and duration statistics
   - Location may continue in the background while recording is in progress (the exact mechanism depends on your device system, such as background location or a foreground service notification)
   - Generate a track share card after recording ends

5. **Card export and sharing**
   - Preview generated results
   - Save images to the device photo library (requires your authorization or follows platform rules)
   - Share to social platforms or other apps through the system share feature

6. **Record management**
   - View historical summit records and track records on a timeline
   - View details for individual records
   - Persist records locally on the device and support deletion

7. **Summit Passport (Pro)**
   - Map overview of summit/place Pins you have visited
   - Aggregate visit points from summit records and track records

8. **Settings and auxiliary features**
   - Altitude units, track distance units, language, haptic feedback, and more
   - Track map theme switching
   - Summit theme color selection
   - Version update detection
   - Obtain weather summaries and related environmental information over the network when needed (with fallback when unavailable)
   - Access to legal documents (Privacy Policy and Terms of Service)

### 2.2 Service Characteristics

- **Local-first**: Arrival records, track records, summit/track cards, and related data are stored on your device by default
- **No registration required**: Core features can be used without creating an account
- **Offline capable**: Altitude and GPS-related features work without a network; features that depend on the network such as reverse geocoding, weather queries, and map loading may degrade or become unavailable
- **Free + Pro**: Core features are free to use (with certain quota limits); Pro membership unlocks Summit Passport, unlimited tracks, unlimited captures/Pins, all theme colors, all card templates, and other extended features. See §2.4
- **Membership benefits are channel-specific**: Pro benefits are tied to the purchase channel and **do not automatically carry over across different app stores or devices**
